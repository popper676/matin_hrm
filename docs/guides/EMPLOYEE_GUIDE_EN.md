# Employee operating guide

Unlock Your Future HR · Release 016

## Your portal and sign-in

1. Sign in with your own account and change a temporary password when prompted. Apps Home shows only the apps the Owner has shared with you.
2. Click an app to open it. Use Search or Favorites, the Back button and Apps Home. On desktop, point to an app for its notes; on a phone, use the info button.
3. Use the account menu for Myanmar/English and light/dark mode. Do not share your login or another person’s private file links.

## Your employee profile

1. Open My Profile to review your employee information and authorized Employee 360 sections. Submit the Employee information form when details need review.
2. An approved profile is separate from a draft change. Salary, job role and app permissions are managed by authorized management.
3. You have one employee account and one monthly salary record in the organization. Business tags only appear when you submit an expense request.

## Check in, check out and attendance

1. Use Check-in with the permitted photo/camera method, or your own QR token if available. Choose a permitted shift and supply any required reason.
2. Check out after work. A very recent scan has a cooldown; failed QR scans do not consume the token. Ask a manager to correct a stale open attendance record.
3. My Attendance shows your records. Workdays and dates use Bangkok time and configured schedules. Overtime requires its separate request and approval.

## Request leave and read your balance

1. Open My Leave and check the category and available balance. Enter the requested dates and full-day/half-day/hourly time when supported.
2. Review the calculated leave units, reason and any handover/coverage requirement, then submit. Submitted approval follows the assigned reviewers.
3. Read conditions and the final decision. Approval conditions may require acknowledgment. If the balance is missing, ask management to check your yearly entitlement.

## Request an expense

1. Open My Rewards & Claims → Request an expense. Choose the business tag for this employee-submitted expense, then enter category, description, purpose, vendor, dates, method, currency, requested amount and reference budget.
2. Attach relevant quotations/receipts (up to five PDF/JPEG/PNG files, 10 MB each). Check the route before submitting. With amount routing, a request over the currency limit or in an unlisted currency requires CEO review.
3. Track requested, approved and actually paid amounts separately. A request is not paid merely because it is approved. Read review conditions, payment details and the PDF.
4. The budget figure is your declared reference, not reserved company funds. Submitted forms are retained; if a correction is needed, ask the reviewer and submit a corrected new form after rejection.

## Daily tasks and proof

1. My Tasks shows all assignments due today and earlier, using the assigned date. Future assignments become visible when due.
2. Complete the checklist or provide the required manual report and proof. Submit the task; the refreshed card shows the saved completion.
3. Read any saved report and proof. If a task/date is wrong, ask the authorized manager to review the assignment rather than marking work you did not perform as complete.

## Your salary and payslip

1. Open My Payslips to view your saved monthly salary, breakdown and Pending/Paid status. Download the authorized payslip PDF when available.
2. The full template separates allowances, overtime, employee deductions and employer contributions. Your salary is consolidated for the whole HR organization, with no per-business split.
3. If a value needs clarification, refer to the month and saved payslip when contacting the authorized payroll person. Your portal cannot edit salary or mark payment.

## Outgoing and incoming handovers

1. Open My Handovers for work you need to pass on, or Incoming handovers for work assigned to you as successor.
2. The outgoing person fills required items, notes and files, then submits. The successor checks current content and acknowledges completed items.
3. Changed content requires a new acknowledgment. Once completed or waived, the saved record becomes read-only. Ask the authorized reviewer if a correction or a new handover is needed.

## Documents, probation and warnings

1. My Documents contains files you are allowed to access. Download through the app; private links can expire and must not be shared with unrelated people.
2. Complete your own probation self-evaluation when invited. Read final feedback and use the available employee response/receipt actions.
3. Read an issued Warning and use comment/acknowledgment as offered. Acknowledgment records receipt rather than agreement. Employee Information changes require their own review.

## Notifications, voting and delegated approvals

1. Use the notification bell to open an assigned action and review the latest status. Read or dismiss your own notifications as appropriate.
2. Peer Voting and other personal apps appear only when shared by the Owner. Monthly voting rejects repeat submissions for the same colleague.
3. If the Owner delegates review duties, use Approvals and act only on the stage assigned to you. Approval access and payment access are distinct. Your own request needs an independent reviewer.
