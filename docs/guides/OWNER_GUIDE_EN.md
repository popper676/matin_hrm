# Owner operating guide

Unlock Your Future HR · Release 016

## Finish the seven setup reviews

1. Open Owner setup from Apps Home. Review Brand identity, Expense business tags, Employees & accounts, Accounts & permissions, Approval responsibilities, Leave calendar & entitlements and Payroll policies.
2. Follow the configuration links and save the real settings in each app. The return-to-setup link takes you back to the same step.
3. After reviewing each step, tick the confirmation and record the review. A relevant settings change reopens the review; the progress count reflects saved configuration.

## Branding and workspace appearance

1. Set Unlock Your Future or your chosen brand name, tagline, accent color and logo in Branding settings. The shared identity appears in both portals and generated forms.
2. Use the account menu for English/Myanmar and light/dark mode. The app launcher is the home screen; use Search, Favorites, Back and Apps Home to move around.
3. On a desktop, hover or focus an app to read its capabilities in the detail area on the right. Every app stays visible, without a floating tooltip. Press Esc to clear the details. Use the info button on touch screens.

## Expense business tags, not employee memberships

1. Business setup is now the Owner's expense business tag catalog. Add or archive names employees can choose when submitting expense requests; existing requests keep their original codes.
2. Employees belong to one HR organization and keep one profile and account. Employee 360 no longer assigns them to individual businesses.
3. One payroll per employee and month; there are no salary business-share percentages or cost allocations. Employee expense requests alone carry a chosen business tag.

## Accounts, privacy and delegated work

1. People & Access lets the Owner choose personal apps, management apps/actions, employee scope and Salary/Bank/Identity fields for each active account. Job titles do not automatically grant access.
2. Give each person only the actions they need: View, Create, Edit, Delete, Approve or Pay where available. Select the employees they may manage. Personal-only access is valid.
3. New employee accounts use temporary passwords that must be changed on first login. Bulk import returns successful account credentials once and lists failed rows; copy each credential privately before leaving the result.

## Amount-based Expense approval

1. Open Approval Setup → Expense. Choose Amount-based approval. Assign the AGM reviewer, an independent CEO reviewer, and a person with Pay access to record payment. Add independent alternates for their own requests.
2. Enter AGM limits for each currency. At or below the limit, the route is AGM → payment. Above it, the route is AGM → CEO → payment. An unlisted currency always requires CEO review. No currency conversion is guessed.
3. Preview a requester and amount, then save the policy. The original requested amount chooses the route; an AGM reduction does not remove a required CEO review. Existing requests retain their route and policy version.
4. Approval and actual payment are separate. The payment step records currency, final amount, payment date, method and reference; it does not execute a bank transfer. Standard mode keeps the original Department → AGM → COO → payment form route.

## Review, conditions and payment

1. Approvals shows requests assigned to you and requests you are permitted to view. Open the original form and attachments before deciding.
2. Approve, approve with conditions, or reject with a reason where supported. Expense authorization may reduce the amount but cannot increase the preceding authorized amount. Payment cannot exceed final approval.
3. Current permissions and account activity are checked again when acting. Your own request requires an independent reviewer. If someone leaves the team, review pending assignments before disabling their account; saved requests retain their original route.

## Leave calendar and entitlement

1. Set company working hours, weekly holidays and special dates in Approval Setup → Leave Calendar. Add shifts/daily schedules or weekday rosters as needed.
2. In Leave, maintain paid/unpaid categories and yearly entitlement for each employee. Leave balances are independent of job titles and expense business tags.
3. Review full-day, half-day or hourly requests using the form’s quoted working time and conditions. Approved leave is retained; the system accounts for configured workdays and overnight shifts.

## Prepare salary and record payment

1. Open Payroll, choose month and employee, and prepare the supplied 23-column Salary Template. Enter approved allowances, OT, SSB, tax, loan and unpaid-leave inputs. Review the calculated net salary before saving Pending.
2. Export saved salary rows to Excel and review employee payslips. One organization maintains exactly one salary record per employee/month without per-business allocations.
3. Record Paid separately with Pay permission. Paid salary is immutable; use a separate adjustment process for corrections. Historical legacy records remain in Saved payroll & settings.
4. The template has its own salary formula and does not automatically add a KPI bonus. Legacy payroll preview/save use their documented KPI bonus rule. SSB, tax and loan inputs are entered amounts, not automated statutory filing or bank transfers.

## Recruitment, JD and attachments

1. Create a job opening with role, job description, required and preferred requirements. Upload supporting JD/requirement files and review extracted text before publishing.
2. Publish the reviewed version to Careers, receive applications, review candidates and record interview decisions. Email invitations require configured SMTP and an explicit send action.
3. Candidate conversion creates a linked employee/account with protected history. AI assessment is optional assistance; check evidence and keep hiring decisions human.

## Onboarding, offboarding and handover

1. Create onboarding for a new hire and track the assigned checklist. Offboarding creates clearance tasks and an exit handover together.
2. Assign a successor. The outgoing employee completes required items; the successor acknowledges current work. Review the submitted version before approving or enter a reason for an authorized waiver.
3. Editing handover content clears prior acknowledgment. Completed/waived history is read-only. Final settlement uses the dedicated release action after the required clearance evidence is complete.

## Official HR forms and documents

1. Employee information, Leave, Expense, Probation and Warning have their own forms and assigned review routes. Read each original form and keep decisions, conditions and receipt separate.
2. Issued documents and reviewed histories are retained. Use the Boss/HR signing sequence where required and share only authorized private downloads.
3. Probation and Warning decisions remain human decisions. An employee acknowledgment records receipt, not agreement. Sensitive salary, bank, identity and private HR notes remain permission-controlled.

## Daily and monthly operations

1. Use Today for assigned approvals, upcoming HR follow-ups, document expiry and unpaid expense follow-up within your permissions.
2. Use Monthly review to compare consolidated payroll changes and missing salary records. Expense requests and receipts can be reviewed separately by selected business tag, currency and payment status.
3. Owner can configure and enable in-app reminders after checking recipients. Review Audit logs when investigating a change. Local notifications require the API server to be running.

## HR assistant and boundaries

1. Ask the HR assistant about data you are authorized to use. AI requires the configured model service and should be checked against the original records.
2. An action proposal does not execute automatically. Read the action card and explicitly confirm only the intended action.
3. This HR system records HR approvals and payments; it does not replace a full accounting ledger, execute bank transfers or decide employment automatically.
